# AdeptAds Google Ads

Build and grow Google Ads campaigns from your website, right in Cursor.

Give Cursor your website, audience, and daily budget. AdeptAds researches keywords, organizes ad groups, and writes ads for Search, Display, or Performance Max campaigns in your Google Ads account.

Already running ads? Ask Cursor which campaigns drove conversions in the last 30 days, then expand one with new keywords, ad groups, or locations.

Free to install. Running a workflow requires an AdeptAds account with trial or paid credits and a connected Google Ads account. Google bills ad spend separately.

## Connect

Install the plugin in Cursor. Sign in to AdeptAds when prompted, connect Google Ads, and choose the account you want to use.
